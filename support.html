Support

How can we help?

Dice Roller VR is a simple touch-controlled 3D dice roller for iPhone and iPad.

Roll the dice

Tap the play surface to start a roll. The dice settle automatically, and the result appears after the throw ends.

Sound and appearance

Use the sound icon to switch dice-contact sound on or off. Open Settings to choose the app language and switch between white and black marble dice.

Use a photo of your table

Tap the camera icon to take a photo of the table beneath your phone. The photo becomes the in-app background for the current session. You can take another photo at any time.

Contact support

For help, feedback, or a privacy question, email samsonin@mail.ru. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

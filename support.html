Support

How can we help?

Dice Roller VR is a simple touch-controlled 3D dice roller for supported Android devices, iPhone, and iPad.

Roll the dice

Tap the play surface to start a roll. The dice settle automatically, and the result appears after the throw ends.

Sound and appearance

Use the sound icon to switch dice-contact sound on or off. Open Settings to choose the app language, dice count and type, and white or black marble dice.

Use a photo of your table

Tap the camera icon to take or select a photo of your table. It becomes the in-app background until you replace it or reset the table. You can choose a new photo at any time.

Contact support

For help, feedback, or a privacy question, email samsonin@mail.ru. Please include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of the issue.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

Dice Roller VR is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or an internet connection. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Camera and table photos

The app asks for camera permission only when you choose to take a photo of your table. The photo is used as the play-surface background during the current app session. It is not uploaded, shared, or used for analytics, and the app does not save it to your photo library.

On-device settings

Your selected language, dice appearance, and sound preference may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember them. These settings never leave your device.

Children

Dice Roller VR does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version and its updated date will be posted on this page.

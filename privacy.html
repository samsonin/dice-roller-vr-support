Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

Dice Roller VR is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or an internet connection. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Camera and table photos

You can choose or take a photo of your table to use as the play-surface background. That photo is kept only in the app's private storage on your device until you replace it or reset the table. It is never uploaded, shared, used for analytics, or added to your photo library by Dice Roller VR.

On-device settings

Your selected language, dice count and type, dice appearance, sound preference, and table background may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember them. These settings never leave your device.

Children

Dice Roller VR does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version and its updated date will be posted on this page.
